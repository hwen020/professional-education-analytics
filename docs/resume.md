**Professional Education Analytics & Workforce Intelligence | Independent portfolio project**

- Built and deployed a Python, SQLite, and Streamlit decision-support dashboard integrating public workforce data, published tuition and statewide and district noncredit education statistics.
- Analyzed year-over-year attendance, enrollment and award trends across independently sourced public series, and produced an executive brief and eight-page case study that documented denominator and data-availability limits.

This portfolio does not establish actual instructor recruiting, service delivery, qualitative fieldwork, institutional impact or use of Asana, Salesforce, Airtable, Lightcast, SIS or financial systems.
