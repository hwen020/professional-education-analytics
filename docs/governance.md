# Delivery and governance

This is a proposed process and workshop agenda. Day numbers are relative to kickoff.

## Policy interpretation

[34 CFR §99.31(a)(1)(i)(A), (a)(1)(ii), and (b)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/subtitle-A/part-99/subpart-D/section-99.31) address school officials with legitimate educational interests, reasonable methods to confine access to qualifying records, and the conditions for release of de-identified education records. Applicability depends on institution and record scope. Review §99.30 for the general consent requirement and confirm applicable institutional rules with the institution's privacy and records owners before any real use. Instructor applicant files require separate HR and records handling. The controls below are proposals.

## Process swimlanes

| Lane | Illustrative current state | Proposed state and handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Program | Emails an unstructured request | Files outcomes, budget and start date. Sends complete packet to Instructor Services. |
| Instructor Services | Tracks outreach and intake through email | Uses rubric, stage timestamps and due dates. Sends approved shortlist to academic reviewer. |
| Academic + Finance | Reviews feasibility and contracts in parallel through email | Approves rubric and staffing. Finance signs scenario and cost ceiling before director gate. |
| Delivery + Privacy | Receives contract and account requests separately | Opens onboarding checklist after signed contract. Privacy reviews access and release separately. |
| Director | Receives an informal status update | Records pilot decision and decision owner. Receives weekly exception summary. |

## Workshop, roles and communication

45-minute workshop agenda. Confirm the current process (10 min), review delay and SLA definitions (10 min), map handoffs (15 min), verify owners, open issues and next gate (10 min).


| Decision | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Metric baseline | Analyst | Program manager | Registrar, Finance | Director |
| Instructor readiness | Instructor Services | Program manager | Academic reviewer, HR | Delivery |
| Data release | Analyst | Privacy lead | Records, HR | Director |
| Pilot spend | Finance | Director | Program manager, Academic | Delivery |

Weekly status goes to lane owners with KPI, exceptions, mitigation and due date. Send urgent items to the accountable owner within one business day. At the gate, send a one-page decision memo to the director for written approval.

## Priority, risks and evaluation

| Priority | Risk or issue | Owner | Trigger and response | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Occupation growth overstates learner demand | Analyst | Sparse local occupation evidence. Gather employer and learner signals before enrolling a cohort. | Day 15 |
| P0 | Cost overrun or weak preorder pipeline | Finance | Paid reservations below the approved break-even threshold or costs above budget. Delay pilot and rerun model. | Day 30 |
| P0 | Exposure through small cells or free text | Privacy | Any identifiable record or denominator below five. Hold release and review disclosure combinations. | Day 20 |
| P1 | Contract delays and license/access gaps | Instructor Services | Review overdue cases weekly. Set named queue and checklist owner. | Day 25 |

Collect a verified baseline at kickoff. Track resolved-ticket SLA and median days from requisition open to hire weekly. Proposed targets are at least 85% resolved-ticket SLA and median fill within 20 calendar days. Analyst compares pilot and baseline by mix and volume at day 60, documents confounders and recommends continue, revise or stop. No observed impact is claimed.

Collect only role, stage and timestamp for mechanics; retain identifiers in a separate approved system if required. Proposed access is role-based with analyst access to de-identified extracts and aggregate-only access for leadership. Propose deletion of temporary extracts within 90 days and validate canonical record schedules and legal holds with Records and HR. Review repeated releases and complementary disclosure; a five-record minimum is an example display rule. The project distributes public market data only.
