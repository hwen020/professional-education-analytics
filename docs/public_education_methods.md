# Public education methods and limitations

| Input | Grain and measurement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| public_cdcp.csv | Fiscal-year noncredit Career Development and College Preparation full-time equivalent students (FTES). First 16 districts in published order plus the statewide total across all districts, 2020–21 to 2024–25. | [Chancellor’s Office, CDCP report, Tables 1–2](https://www.cccco.edu/-/media/CCCCO-Website/docs/report/2020-25-cdcp-report-a11y.pdf?hash=BC3F663F1D304721242B90395B7F88A352CFD96C&la=en) |
| public_calbright.csv | College-wide headcount on June 30 and award completions during each calendar year, 2020–2025. | [California Legislative Analyst’s Office, Figure 11](https://lao.ca.gov/Publications/Report/5150#figure11) |

The records are manual transcriptions of official aggregate tables. Published zeros are retained. YoY is current divided by prior minus one, with a blank first period and a blank rate whenever the base is zero. Absolute FTES change is rounded to source precision. The district sample follows publication order and provides a comparison set, with no representativeness claim. The statewide series overlaps district values. The PivotTable disables grand totals across geography and time.

FTES measures attendance workload in equivalent students. June 30 headcount is a stock and calendar-year awards are a flow. These observations do not support a cohort completion rate, a quarterly or monthly trend, or a forecast. The provider series describes one public college and cannot quantify demand for the three proposed courses. Program-level capacity, applicant and withdrawal counts, net tuition, unit economics, individual recruitment, instructor supply, SLA and interview or survey data are unavailable in the selected public sources. No Program Health Score or impact estimate is assigned.

The market forecasts and wages, public education observations and editable hypothetical pilot inputs have separate worksheets and dashboard views. The original market snapshot and PDFs retain their prior scope. The updated [source registry](../data/education_sources.csv) preserves tables, dates, units and links.
