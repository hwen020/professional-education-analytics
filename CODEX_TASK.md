# Codex implementation brief
Extend this existing prototype in place, not a new application. Final public repository: professional-education-analytics. Target: decision-support portfolio for continuing education, not an institutional system. English UI and deliverables. Preserve existing functionality; no private data, real university branding, affiliation claims, or fabricated findings.

## P0: evidence and deliverables
1. Retrieve public BLS occupational projections and wages, California EDD regional labor market data, O*NET skills and published continuing education course offerings/prices. Record URLs, retrieval dates, geography, years, units, licenses and cached snapshots where allowed. Map SOC codes explicitly; do not conflate annual openings with job postings. Missing sources must remain missing, not simulated as real. Compare at least three candidate programs and document selection criteria, demand, competition, skill alignment, price and uncertainty. Do not claim Lightcast access.
2. Expand seeded synthetic operational data to 8 quarters, multiple programs/cohorts, enrollment and outcomes, instructor applicant events, service tickets and qualitative feedback. Use anonymous IDs, constraints, data dictionary and provenance. Build SQL or DuckDB views and reproducible Python analysis. Validate grain, duplicates, missingness, date order, denominators, reconciliation, small-cell privacy and funnel monotonicity. Separate synthetic and public data visually and in exports.
3. Portfolio analysis: lifecycle stage, enrollment trend, capacity utilization, withdrawals, completion, revenue, direct cost, contribution margin, variance vs target, risks and recommended actions. Define every KPI and distinguish contribution from profit. Add cohort and modality filters, weighted aggregate rates, uncertainty and limitations.
4. Instructor analysis: sourcing, outreach, screening, interview, offers, hire, time-to-fill, stage dwell time, channel conversion and bottleneck; service ticket SLA, backlog, aging, escalation, onboarding and workload. No real outreach. Add qualitative thematic coding, codebook, sample selection, counts, illustrative quotes and triangulation with quantitative findings.
5. Business case: transparent weighted scoring, competitor scan, demand and skills mapping, low/base/high enrollment scenarios, break-even, sensitivity to price/cost, risks, go/no-go and decision gates. Distinguish forecast assumptions from observations.
6. Governance: research actual published applicable higher-education policy and cite exact sections; distinguish institutional rules from proposed controls. Draft as-is/to-be swimlanes, workshop agenda and mock stakeholder notes explicitly labeled, RACI, handoffs, controls, process metrics, milestones, dependencies, priority register, risk/issue log, stakeholder communication, implementation and evaluation plan. Use baseline and proposed targets, never claim observed improvements. Add student/instructor data minimization, access and retention plan; public demo has no PII.
7. Generate a 1–2 page executive brief PDF, 8–12 page integrated report PDF, and advanced Excel workbook with formula-driven KPIs, editable assumptions, scenario formulas, real pivot tables if supported (otherwise clearly label summary tables), charts, source registry and QA checks. All figures and conclusions derive from the same reproducible analysis. Export CSV and a project task tracker compatible with Asana; provide generic CRM/Airtable-style schema and CSV imports, not claims of live integrations. Include action owners and decision deadlines. Use concise professional English. Verify PDF rendering and Excel formula recalculation.

## P1: product and validation
Extend Streamlit with portfolio, instructor recruitment/services, qualitative insights, market intelligence, scenario analysis and delivery/governance pages; consistent filters and downloads, accessible responsive layout. Public demo read-only except local ephemeral scenario controls. Add requirements lock, Dockerfile, CI, reproducible data pipeline, tests for all KPI calculations and edge cases, dashboard smoke tests, export reconciliation and privacy checks. Publish only after actual tests pass; deploy to Render if authorized, verify public desktop/mobile, otherwise document remaining blocker. No unnecessary authentication, microservices or LLM features.
